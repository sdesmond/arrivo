# Epic 2 Retrospective: Role-Scoped Trip Access

## Findings

### Critical — a viewer's trip budget is not stripped from the `/trips` and `/travel-history` list payloads

`getTripDetail` (`src/lib/queries.ts:494`) correctly calls `dbTripToType(trip, { role: accessRole })`, so the trip-detail page's viewer path omits `budget`/`expenses`/per-event `expense` exactly as story 2.1 specifies. `listTripsForUser` (`src/lib/queries.ts:213`), which feeds both `/trips` (`src/app/(app)/trips/page.tsx`) and `/travel-history` (`src/app/(app)/travel-history/page.tsx`), resolves the same `accessRole` two lines earlier (line 209) and uses it to stamp `trip.accessRole` for UI gating (line 235) — but never passes `{ role: accessRole }` into `dbTripToType(trip)` at line 213. A viewer's real `budget.total`/`budget.currency` is therefore serialized into the RSC payload handed to `TripsPageClient`/`TravelHistoryPageClient` for every trip where the user is only a viewer, reachable via network/devtools inspection regardless of whether any current list card renders it.

This directly contradicts the epic's own declared goal ("no money data anywhere they can reach it") and story 2.1's own acceptance criterion ("any event, reservation, or itinerary item that carries a cost/price-shaped field... omitted... checked against every field currently defined in `src/data/types.ts`, not assumed absent" — `Trip.budget` is exactly such a field). It was not an accident nobody thought about: story 2.1's own Design Notes states "only trip-detail passes role, keeping dashboard/spending lists byte-identical" — a deliberate scope choice made while implementing 2.1, never surfaced as a risk to the epic or flagged for stakeholder sign-off, and never revisited by story 2.4 (which added viewer-exclusion for the *spending aggregate* fed by a different query, `listExpensesForUserInRange`) or by story 2.2's list-page review pass (which added `accessRole` to the same `Trip` type for a different purpose — gating the Delete button — without noticing the sibling gap in the same file).

- **Source:** `src/lib/queries.ts:209-235` (no role passed) vs. `src/lib/queries.ts:494` (role passed); `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-2-1-viewer-gets-a-read-only-financial-data-free-trip-view.md` Design Notes; `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/epic-2-context.md` Goal section.
- **Verified independently by:** the adversarial, edge-case, and verification-gap review lenses (run in parallel over the epic diff), and confirmed directly by hand-reading `queries.ts:209-235` and its callers.
- **Test coverage:** none. `queries.test.ts`'s `listTripsForUser` describe block has zero assertions mentioning `budget`; `viewer-trip-access.feature`'s three scenarios only visit the trip-detail page.
- **Disposition:** fix-now.

### Critical — "Edit trip" is ungated by role on every list-page card, reopening the same leak in the UI itself

All six list-page trip-card components (`trip-card.tsx`, `now-card.tsx`, `mobile-past-tab.tsx`, `trips-desktop-layout.tsx`, `unscheduled-section.tsx`, `trips-past-list.tsx`) were correctly updated in story 2.2's expanded scope to gate their "Delete trip" `DropdownMenuItem` on `trip.accessRole === CollaboratorRole.Manager`. None of the six apply any equivalent gate to the neighboring "Edit trip" item — e.g. `trip-card.tsx:74-83` renders "Edit trip" unconditionally while `:84` gates "Delete trip" on `canManage` two lines later. Clicking "Edit trip" from any list page opens `TripEditSheet`, which renders `<TripEditBudget>` (`trip-edit-sheet.tsx:247`) unconditionally — only the People `<Section>` at line 260 is gated on `canManage`. A viewer can therefore reach the trip's live budget fields (and an apparently-editable form for them) from every list surface, even though `trip-detail-hero.tsx` — the sibling entry point to the identical `TripEditSheet`, touched in the same epic — correctly hides its own Edit trigger for viewers via `canEdit = accessRole !== CollaboratorRole.Viewer` (`trip-detail-hero.tsx:70,159-171`).

The write itself is currently inert (`updateTripMetadata` still denies at `assertTripAccess(tripId, userId, "editor")`, `trips/[tripId]/actions.ts:364`), but the *read* — a viewer seeing the real budget number rendered in a form — is exactly what story 2.1 exists to prevent, and it is currently reachable through a control every story-2.2 reviewer looked directly at while fixing its sibling button.

- **Source:** `src/components/packt/trip-card.tsx:74-96`, `now-card.tsx:126-138`, `mobile-past-tab.tsx:69-79`, `trips-desktop-layout.tsx:170-183`, `unscheduled-section.tsx:83-90`, `trips-past-list.tsx:100-109` (no Edit-trip gate in any of the six) vs. `trip-detail-hero.tsx:70,159-174` (same action, correctly gated) and `trip-edit-sheet.tsx:247-254` (`TripEditBudget` unconditional; only `:260` People section is `canManage`-gated).
- **Verified independently by:** all three review lenses, and confirmed directly by hand-reading each file.
- **Test coverage:** none. Every role-based test added across the six components (`trip-card.test.tsx:156`, `mobile-past-tab.test.tsx:117-126`, `trips-desktop-layout.test.tsx:387`, `unscheduled-section.test.tsx:158-176`, `trips-past-list.test.tsx:151-161`, `now-card.test.tsx:197`) is `it.each([Editor, Viewer])("hides Delete trip for a %s", ...)` — none assert Edit-trip's presence/absence by role.
- **Disposition:** fix-now, alongside the finding above — both close the same "no money data anywhere reachable" gap from its two remaining open sides (payload and UI-entry-point).

### Moderate — `updateCollaboratorRole` structurally cannot promote to manager, but the UI it will soon drive says it can

`updateCollaboratorRole(collabId: string, role: "editor" | "viewer")` (`trips/actions.ts:928`) excludes `"manager"` from its type. `trip-edit-collaborators.tsx:84-86`'s role `<Select>` for every non-creator row offers Manager/Editor/Viewer, and story 2.3 established that non-creator managers are full peers ("The creator can still remove and demote OTHER managers — only the creator's own row is protected"), implying two-way role changes including promotion to manager. The People section is currently known-dead UI (draft-state only, per story 2.3's own Spec Change Log), so nothing breaks today — but the mismatch is baked in now and will surface the moment Epic 3 wires this section to live actions, exactly where `deferred-work.md` already tracks a closely related gap (the same action's missing runtime role validation, `trips/actions.ts:928`).

- **Source:** `src/app/(app)/trips/actions.ts:928` vs. `src/components/packt/trip-edit-collaborators.tsx:84-86` and `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-2-3-manager-has-full-trip-control.md` I/O matrix.
- **Disposition:** defer — fold into the existing `deferred-work.md` entry for `updateCollaboratorRole`, to be resolved alongside Epic 3's People-section wiring, not before.

### Accepted, pre-existing — viewer-exclusion join keys on `userId`, not the table's actual unique constraint

Already recorded in `deferred-work.md` ("Deferred from: code review of spec-2-4"): `listExpensesForUserInRange`'s new `ne(collaborators.role, "viewer")` filter (`queries.ts:257`) joins on `(tripId, userId)`, but `collaborators`' unique constraint is `(tripId, email)` — a user with two collaborator rows on one trip (different emails, same linked `userId`) could have the join match the non-viewer row and bypass the exclusion. Confirmed by this retro to compose directly with the finding above: it is not a hypothetical edge case but the same class of "financial data reaches a viewer" gap, just via a different query. Already deferred and correctly attributed as pre-existing (not introduced by story 2.4's diff) — accepted as-is for this retro, tracked where it already is.

- **Source:** `deferred-work.md`; `src/lib/queries.ts:257`.
- **Disposition:** accept as-is (already tracked correctly); no new action item, cross-referenced from the fix-now items above since a fix to the finding above should re-check this composition.

### Noted, not actioned — minor payload-shape change on the `expense` key for non-viewer roles

The diff changed `expense: hasExpense ? dbExpenseToType(...) : undefined` (pre-epic-2) to a conditional spread `...(!isViewer && hasExpense ? { expense: ... } : {})` across reservations/day-events/unscheduled-events. For a non-viewer with no linked expense, the `expense` key is now omitted entirely rather than present-with-`undefined`. Checked and found inconsequential: `JSON.stringify`/RSC serialization already drop `undefined`-valued keys, so the wire payload is identical either way; only in-memory `'expense' in obj` / `Object.keys(obj)` checks would observe a difference, and no consumer in the codebase does so. No action needed.

## Behavior verification

Runtime behavior was not exercised end-to-end via browser (no `run`/Playwright session in this retro) — verification here is direct source inspection, which is stronger than a black-box pass for this kind of finding since the defect is in what data reaches the client, not in rendering. Traced and confirmed by hand:

- `assertTripAccess` (`src/lib/auth-helpers.ts:23-38`): single rank-based chokepoint (`viewer=0, editor=1, manager=2`), denies via `notFound()` before any caller code runs. Correct.
- `getTripDetail`'s viewer path (`queries.ts:338-551`): budget, flat `expenses`, and every per-event/per-reservation `expense` field are correctly omitted for `isViewer`, across day events, unscheduled events, and reservations (including `linkedEvents`, which derives from the already-stripped `reservationsDomain`). Matches story 2.1's AC exactly.
- `removeCollaborator`/`updateCollaboratorRole` `isCreator` guards (`trips/actions.ts:916-935`): correctly reject any change targeting `isCreator: true`, checked after `assertTripAccess(..., "manager")`, regardless of caller identity. Matches story 2.3's AC exactly.
- Manager-only action gates (`deleteTrip`, `addCollaborator`, `removeCollaborator`, `updateCollaboratorRole`) all call `assertTripAccess(..., "manager")`; every itinerary/budget mutation action across `trips/[tripId]/actions.ts`, `budget/actions.ts`, `reservations/actions.ts`, etc. gates at `"editor"`. Comprehensive — no missing call site found across ~35 action functions checked.
- `trip-detail-hero.tsx`'s Share button is correctly nested inside `canManage` (not just `canEdit`) — matches story 2.2's AC.
- The list-page Delete-affordance gate (`trip.accessRole === CollaboratorRole.Manager`) is applied identically and correctly across all six touched list components.
- **Not verified in a live browser:** the two critical findings above (list-page budget leak, ungated Edit-trip button) — recommend a quick manual check (open `/trips` as a viewer, inspect the RSC payload; click "Edit trip" as a viewer) before or immediately after the fix lands, to close the loop this retro's source-only verification left open.
