- (direction by user) Creative Partner mode; start with Job to Be Done, then coach chooses two fitting techniques.
- (decision by coach) Selected Constraint Mapping and Decision Tree Mapping after Job to Be Done to expose permission boundaries and invitation lifecycle choices.
- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (insight by user) Collaboration replaces shared logins and being physically together, enabling asynchronous remote planning and keeping non-participants informed of plans.
- (idea by coach) Underlying job: give every interested person a current shared itinerary without sharing credentials or coordinating a meeting.
- (question by coach) If collaboration worked perfectly, what first thing would an invited person do differently after opening the trip?
- (insight by user) Clarified that invited people do not exist today; prior question was ambiguous.
- (question by coach) After an owner sends an invite and recipient opens the trip, what first useful outcome should recipient get?
- (idea by user) A future message center could surface sharing notifications.
- (idea by user) Both recipient and owner need visible indicators that a trip is shared.
- (insight by coach) The feature has separate jobs: invite discovery, shared-state awareness, and collaboration itself; they can be phased independently.
- (question by coach) Describe ideal first screen a recipient sees after accepting an invite.
- (idea by user) Recipient accepts invite from inbox and lands directly on newly shared trip overview.
- (idea by coach) Trip overview can state who shared it and recipient access level, making shared status and capabilities clear at point of use.
- (question by coach) What must recipient immediately understand from overview to act confidently?
- (decision by user) Recipient overview must make trip identity, which trips are shared, and recipient access level explicit.
- (technique) started Constraint Mapping
- (question by coach) What risky or irreversible actions must an editor never be able to do?
- (decision by user) Editors can edit itinerary but cannot delete trip or share it with others.
- (insight by coach) Editor boundary: plan content is mutable; trip existence and membership are protected.
- (question by coach) Should manager be able to delete a trip, or only invite/remove people and change their access levels?
- (decision by user) Manager and owner are same full-access role, including deletion and membership management.
- (question by coach) What may a viewer do beyond reading the shared itinerary, if anything?
- (decision by user) Viewer is read-only; may download future PDF export but has no other actions.
- (insight by coach) Permission model can express capabilities: viewer = read and export; editor = viewer plus itinerary mutation; manager = all trip and membership actions.
- (question by coach) Must recipient already have Packt account before an invite is sent, or can invite create a sign-up path?
- (decision by user) Invite supports recipient sign-up path.
- (idea by coach) Email-specific invite can preserve intended access through signup, then route account holder to accept and open trip overview.
- (question by coach) If an invite link is forwarded, may an account registered to another email accept it?
- (decision by user) Invite links bind to intended email; an account with another email cannot accept a forwarded link.
- (technique) started Decision Tree Mapping
- (question by coach) How should manager begin sharing: enter recipient email, select an existing Packt user, or use both?
- (decision by user) Preferred share entry: find existing user by email, which uniquely identifies Packt users.
- (idea by user) Future friend list lets managers pick known people instead of entering email.
- (insight by coach) One email-first flow can handle both branches: existing user receives in-app invite; no account receives email signup invite.
- (question by coach) Which role should new invitation preselect, and may manager change it before sending?
- (decision by user) Email is sole invitation entry flow for now; no friends picker.
- (decision by user) Existing email match gets in-app share invitation; unmatched email gets email invite with signup route.
- (question by coach) When manager enters email, what access level should invitation form show initially?
- (decision by user) New invitation defaults to viewer access.
- (question by coach) May manager change a contributor access level after invite is accepted?
- (decision by user) Managers may change contributor access level after acceptance.
- (question by coach) When manager removes a contributor, should trip disappear immediately from contributor view and shared-trip list?
- (decision by user) Removing contributor immediately revokes access and removes trip from contributor view and shared-trip list.
- (question by user) Asked which technique batch was complete.
- (direction by user) Continue generating ideas; make technique transitions explicit.
- (decision by coach) Next batch: Role Playing, Assumption Reversal, and Failure Analysis to expose stakeholder needs, challenge defaults, and test harmful edges.
- (technique) started Role Playing
- (insight by user) Viewer wants daily activity visibility, fears losing track of traveler location, and demands complete accurate up-to-date information.
- (idea by coach) Editor stakeholder wants independent plan changes, fears accidental damage, and needs access level plus editable controls to be unmistakable.
- (question by coach) Speak as manager: what do they want, fear, and demand when they share trip?
- (insight by user) Manager wants easy sharing, fears role enforcement errors, and demands up-to-date trip information during concurrent contributor edits.
- (insight by coach) Correct server-enforced permissions and visibility of fresh cross-user changes are product trust requirements, not minor UI details.
- (question by coach) When two editors change same itinerary item nearly simultaneously, what outcome should Packt favor?
- (decision by user) Concurrent edits use latest-write-wins behavior.
- (technique) started Assumption Reversal
- (question by coach) Reverse email-invite-only sharing: what valuable capability, if any, appears when someone can request access to a trip?
- (decision by user) No access-request flow for private trips; it requires public discovery and creates privacy risk. Access stays manager-issued.
- (insight by coach) Private-by-default trips and manager-issued invitations form the collaboration trust boundary; public discoverability is separate scope.
- (question by coach) Reverse permanent access: should contributor access automatically end after trip finishes, or persist until manager removes it?
- (decision by user) Contributor access persists after trip until manager removes it.
- (decision by user) Trips shared with viewer access do not appear in reports, including budget reports.
- (question by coach) What other trip information or surfaces should viewer access hide, if any?
- (decision by user) Viewer access hides all money and budget information.
- (question by coach) Should editor see and edit money/budget information, or is that manager-only?
- (decision by user) Editors can see and edit money and budget items; restriction applies only to viewers.
- (technique) started Failure Analysis
- (question by coach) What failure would most seriously destroy trust in sharing, and what must prevent it?
- (decision by user) Out-of-date or incorrect shared trip information is catastrophic; synchronization between sessions is critical.
- (question by coach) What freshness guarantee do you expect: changes appear live without reload, or reliably after refresh/navigation/action?
- (decision by user) Shared trip changes must appear live across sessions without reload.
- (question by coach) If live synchronization disconnects, what should user see or be prevented from doing until it returns?
- (decision by user) Show an offline/internet-down indicator in status bar when live synchronization cannot operate.
- (question by coach) What should happen to someone actively viewing a trip when manager removes their access?
- (decision by user) Live access removal shows modal stating access is revoked, directs recipient to contact named trip owner for resharing, and OK returns to own dashboard; back navigation must not reopen trip.
- (question by coach) What recovery should manager have after sending invitation to wrong person or wrong access level?
- (decision by user) Manager contributor list supports removing person and changing access level through dropdown, recovering from wrong recipient or role.
- (decision by coach) Final divergence batch: How Might We, Morphological Analysis, and Backcasting for opportunities, configuration coverage, and target experience.
- (technique) started How Might We
- (idea by user) Share dialog has top email input for new people and list of shared people with role dropdowns and trash removal icons, making permissions visible at a glance.
- (insight by coach) One share dialog serves invite, audit, role update, and removal without hiding access state across screens.
- (question by coach) How might we make role changes understandable immediately to contributor whose screen updates live?
- (decision by user) Share dialog stages role/removal changes and requires Save or Cancel; it cannot dismiss otherwise because permissions are security-sensitive.
- (technique) started Morphological Analysis
- (question by coach) Before invite is accepted, where should manager see pending invite and what control should they have over it?
- (decision by user) Pending invites appear in share dialog with same role-change and delete controls as active contributors.
- (decision by user) Accepted invite grants role current at acceptance, not role at send; if removed before acceptance, recipient sees rescinded-invite message.
- (question by coach) May manager send another pending invite to same email, or should existing pending row be the only invitation to edit or remove?
- (decision by user) Managers can resend a pending invitation.
- (question by coach) Should pending invitation expire automatically after a set time?
- (decision by user) Pending invitations expire after 72 hours; possible configurability is open.
- (question by coach) If configurable, should expiry be a per-invite manager choice or a global application setting?
- (decision by user) Invitation expiry is global application setting, not manager or per-invite choice.
- (technique) started Backcasting
- (question by coach) Imagine a group trip ended with sharing working perfectly: what concrete evidence proves feature succeeded?
- (insight by user) Success: all contributors track trip progress, view events and locations, and stay synchronized.
- (question by coach) What needs to be true when trip begins to make that outcome inevitable?
- (insight by user) Trip-start prerequisite is sharing working as described; successful sharing is core foundation.
- (insight by coach) Backcast foundation is correct membership/permissions plus reliable live synchronization; other sharing UI builds on it.
- (question by coach) Which smallest first slice must exist before any sharing UI can safely ship?
- (decision by user) Smallest first release: share dialog, select a user to receive view-only access, and recipient successfully views trip.
- (technique) convergence: MoSCoW scope sorting
- (insight by coach) Convergence tension: live synchronization is declared critical, while smallest first release names only view-only sharing; needs explicit release-scope decision.
- (decision by user) MoSCoW: move live cross-session synchronization into Must scope because stale shared data destroys trust.
- (decision by coach) Converged scope: Must includes viewer sharing foundation plus live synchronization; roles beyond viewer are sequenced after foundation.
- (insight by user) Share dialog is hub for all contributor functionality.
- (insight by coach) Share dialog is collaboration control plane: it unifies invite delivery/state, role changes, revocation, resend, and trustworthy audit; explicit Save/Cancel is its security boundary while server-side authorization and live sync enforce outcomes elsewhere.
