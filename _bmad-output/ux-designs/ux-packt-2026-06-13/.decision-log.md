- 2026-06-13: "Linked" color tokens — new indigo/violet pair: `--linked-brand` (~#5b5fc7 light / ~#8b8ef0 dark), `--linked-soft` (row tint), `--linked-ink` (badge text), mirroring how `--accent-soft`/`--accent-ink` are derived from `--accent-brand`. Used for the Linked Event left border + row tint + "Linked" badge variant (FR-7, FR-11). Distinct from teal (now-highlight) and emerald (success).
