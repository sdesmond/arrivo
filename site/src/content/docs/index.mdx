<FeatureRow
  eyebrow="Itinerary"
  title="A day-by-day plan that keeps up with you"
  screenshot="trip-day-expanded"
  alt="An expanded itinerary day showing an event with its time, place, cost and attachment options"
  href="/arrivo/guide/day-planning/"
  linkText="Plan your days"
>
  Every day is a card. Add events with a time, place, cost and note, attach documents, and drag them into order.

- Arrivo flags events scheduled too close together for the travel between them.
- A day with two or more places shows the route through them.
- Events that lose their date when you change a trip's dates wait in **Unscheduled Events**, not lost.
- While you travel, **Trips** shows what is happening now and what comes next.

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow
  reverse
  eyebrow="Reservations"
  title="Enter a booking once. It shows up where you need it."
  screenshot="trip-reservations"
  alt="The Reservations tab with Lodging and Flight groups, each showing the next booking"
  href="/arrivo/guide/day-planning/#reservations"
  linkText="How reservations work"
>
  Flights, lodging and other bookings live in one tab, grouped by type with the soonest first.

- Lodging appears on the itinerary as check-in and check-out on the right days.
- A flight that leaves and lands on the same day shows as one linked event.
- A booking outside your trip dates adds the day it needs.
- Attach the confirmation right to the reservation.

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow
  eyebrow="Budget"
  title="Know where the money is going"
  screenshot="trip-budget"
  alt="The Budget tab with total spent, a warning badge, category totals and an expense list"
  href="/arrivo/guide/spending/"
  linkText="Track spending"
>
  Set a budget for a trip, add costs to events or as miscellaneous expenses, and see the total and each category at a glance.

- The status badge changes at the warning and danger percentages you choose in your preferences.
- **Travel History → Spending** adds up spending across all your trips, with charts by category and destination.
- Set your default currency in your preferences.

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow
  reverse
  eyebrow="Sharing"
  title="Plan together, with the right access"
  screenshot="trip-share"
  alt="The Share trip dialog with an invite field, a collaborator, a pending invitation and a declined invitation"
  href="/arrivo/guide/sharing/"
  linkText="Share a trip"
>
  Invite people by username and choose what they can do. Viewers can read a trip, editors plan it, and managers also control who is on it.

- Invitations are pending until accepted, and they expire.
- A role change applies right away, even to someone who has the trip open.
- Edits from other people appear live, without a reload.

</FeatureRow>
