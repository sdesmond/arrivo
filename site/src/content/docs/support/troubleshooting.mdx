## I can't sign in

- Check your username, not your email address.
- If you use two-factor, enter the current code from your authenticator app, or a recovery code (each works once).
- Forgot your password? Ask your admin for a reset link.

## I lost my phone and my recovery codes

Ask your admin to start recovery for your account. Admins: see [Recovering an account](/arrivo/self-hosting/#recovering-an-account).

## An admin lost their password, phone or recovery codes

See [An admin lost their password, phone or recovery codes](/arrivo/self-hosting/#an-admin-lost-their-password-phone-or-recovery-codes).

## The page shows a sync warning

Arrivo keeps the open trip up to date in the background. A reconnecting icon means the connection dropped. It clears when the connection recovers. If it says it can't sync, check your network and the server.

## An upload is rejected

Only PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC files up to 25 MB are accepted.

## Cover image search doesn't work

Photo search needs an Unsplash key on the server. Ask your admin, or see [Self-hosting](/arrivo/self-hosting/#configuration).

## Server problems

Starting, updating and backing up the server: see [Self-hosting → Troubleshooting](/arrivo/self-hosting/#troubleshooting).
