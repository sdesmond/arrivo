## Who can see my trips?

Only you and the people you share them with. Documents are private to the trip. Admins manage accounts and the server, not your trips.

## Where is my data stored?

On the server running Arrivo: trips in its PostgreSQL database, and uploaded documents and covers in its storage folder. There is no hosted Arrivo service. If your admin enables map and cover-photo search, those lookups go to Mapbox and Unsplash. See [Self-hosting](/arrivo/self-hosting/).

## Why is the Map tab empty?

The server has no valid Mapbox token. Ask your admin to set `MAPBOX_ACCESS_TOKEN`. See [Map](/arrivo/guide/map/).

## What files can I upload?

PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC, up to 25 MB each.

## I can't open the Share dialog or see the Budget tab.

Sharing is for managers, and Budget is hidden from viewers. Ask a manager of the trip to change your role. See [Roles and permissions](/arrivo/reference/roles/).

## Can I plan a trip without dates?

Yes. Leave the dates empty and add them later. The trip sits under **Unscheduled** until it has dates.

## My invitation disappeared.

Invitations expire. Ask the trip's manager to resend it.

## I lost my two-factor device.

Sign in with one of your recovery codes (each works once). If you have none left, ask your admin. See [Troubleshooting](/arrivo/support/troubleshooting/).

## How do I reset a password?

Ask your admin for a reset link.
