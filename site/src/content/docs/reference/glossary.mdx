---
title: Glossary
description: Terms used in Arrivo.
---

**Active, Upcoming, Past**
: A trip's status, worked out from its dates and today's date. A trip with no dates counts as upcoming and sits in **Unscheduled**.

**Event**
: A stop or activity on a day: it can have a note, time, place, cost and attachments.

**Linked event**
: An itinerary entry created from a reservation, such as hotel Check-in or a flight. Edit it in Reservations.

**Reservation**
: A booking, such as lodging or a flight, kept on the Reservations tab.

**Unscheduled events**
: Events kept aside when they no longer belong to a day, for example after trip dates change.

**Collaborator**
: Someone you've shared a trip with. They have a role: viewer, editor or manager.

**Pending invitation**
: A share invite the person hasn't accepted yet. It expires.

**Template**
: A reusable packing list saved in Packing Templates.

**Admin**
: A person who manages the Arrivo server. Not the same as a trip manager.
