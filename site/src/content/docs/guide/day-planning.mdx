The **Itinerary** tab lists one card per day. Each card shows the date, a short summary of the stops, and how many stops it has. Today's card is marked **Active**.

<Screenshot name="trip-day-expanded" alt="An expanded itinerary day with an event, its time, place, cost and attachment options, and a note" />

## Events

Open a day to see its events. For each event you can add a note, a time, a place, a cost, and an attachment. Events you haven't scheduled, for example after shortening a trip's dates, are kept in an **Unscheduled Events** group at the top of the itinerary so nothing is lost.

Drag events to reorder them within a day.

## Reservations

Flights, lodging and other bookings live in the **Reservations** tab, grouped by type with the soonest first.

<Screenshot name="trip-reservations" alt="The Reservations tab with Lodging and Flight groups" />

Reservations also show up on the itinerary as linked events, so you don't enter them twice:

- A lodging reservation appears as **Check-in** and **Check-out** on the right days.
- A flight that leaves and lands on the same day appears as one linked event.
- Selecting a linked event opens it in Reservations.
- A reservation outside the trip's dates adds a day to the trip. If you later shorten the trip past a reservation, Arrivo warns you and lets you keep it.

## Schedule conflicts

When two timed events with places are scheduled closer together than the travel time between them, Arrivo flags a conflict on the day. Select the day's conflict button to jump to the first conflicting event. Events without a time or place aren't flagged themselves.

## Route strip

A day with two or more places shows a route strip, so you can see the order you'll move through them.

## Notes

The **Notes** tab holds notes for the whole trip, such as a rail-pass reminder or backup restaurants. Add them with **New Note**, drag to reorder, and delete with the trash icon.

<Screenshot name="trip-notes" alt="The Notes tab with two trip notes" />

## Live updates

If someone else edits the trip while you have it open, their changes appear without reloading.
