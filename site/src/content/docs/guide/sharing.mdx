## Invite someone

Open a trip and choose **Share**. Start typing in the invite field and choose the person from the list of registered users, pick a role, and choose **Invite**. They must already have an Arrivo account on your server; the list leaves out you, people already on the trip, people with a pending invitation and accounts that have not finished setting a password. People who declined or were cancelled can be invited again. If you are the only user, the dialog links to the Users screen where an admin can add more.

The dialog lists everyone on the trip:

- **People with access**, with a role you can change, or remove with the trash icon. You can't change your own row.
- **Pending** invitations, with their expiry. Resend or cancel one. An expired invitation can still be resent or cancelled.
- **Declined** invitations, which you can dismiss.

Only managers can open the sharing dialog. See [Roles and permissions](/arrivo/reference/roles/) for what each role can do.

## When you're invited

A badge on **Trips** in the sidebar counts your pending invitations. Open **Trips** to **accept** (you go straight to the trip) or **decline**. Expired invitations disappear from your list.

## Leave a trip

From the trip's card menu, choose **Leave trip** and confirm. The creator and managers can't leave.

## Changes apply right away

If your role changes while you have the trip open, the page updates: for example, someone moved to viewer loses the Budget tab immediately.
