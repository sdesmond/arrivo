**Packing Templates** in the sidebar is your library of reusable lists.

<Screenshot name="packing-templates" alt="The empty Packing Templates page with a Create first template button" />

## Create and edit a template

Choose **New template**, then open it to add plain items and section labels. You can edit or remove items, rename or delete a label (its items stay), and drag to reorder.

## Save a list as a template

From a trip's packing list, save it under a name. The saved template keeps your items but unchecks them, and the original list is unchanged.

## Pull a template into a trip

An empty list offers **Pull from template** in its overflow menu. You can also pull a template onto a list that already has items: the new items are added after the existing ones, and items that match are skipped so you don't get duplicates, even when you pull several templates.
