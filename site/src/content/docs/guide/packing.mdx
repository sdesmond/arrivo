---
title: Packing lists
description: Keep one or more packing lists for each trip.
---

import Screenshot from "../../../components/Screenshot.astro";

The **Packing** tab holds a trip's packing lists. Choose **New List** to create one, then open it to add items and check them off. Each list shows how many items it has.

<Screenshot name="trip-packing" alt="The Packing tab with two lists, Essentials and Gear" />

To skip retyping, [pull a template](/arrivo/guide/templates/) into a list, or [save a list as a template](/arrivo/guide/templates/#save-a-list-as-a-template) for the next trip.

Packing progress also appears on past trips in [Travel history](/arrivo/guide/travel-history/), for example **4 / 4 packed**.
