Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**. The page is called Profile & Settings and has sections for Account, Traveler, Companions, Preferences, Appearance and Security. Account holds your name and username, a **Change password** button that opens a dialog (it shows the password rules as you type, and signs you out everywhere when you finish), and **Delete account**.

## Profile photo

Hover over your photo (or tab to it) and choose **Upload photo**, or **Replace photo** if you already have one. Pick a JPG, PNG or WEBP image up to 5 MB. Arrivo crops it to a square and shrinks it, so a phone photo works as it is. Choose **Remove photo** under the picture to go back to your initials.

Your photo shows in the sidebar, on trip cards, and next to your name when someone shares a trip with you. Anyone signed in to your Arrivo can see it, so use a picture you are happy to share with the people on the same server. If a photo can't load, Arrivo shows your initials instead.

## Preferences

<Screenshot
  name="profile-preferences"
  alt="The Preferences section with date format, currency, distance units, default cover type, budget thresholds and transit settings"
/>

- **Date format**: Month/Day or Day/Month. It applies across your trips.
- **Currency** and **distance units** (Imperial or Metric).
- **Default trip cover type**: Photo, Map or Graphic, used for new trips.
- **Budget**: the **Warning %** and **Danger %** at which a trip's [budget](/arrivo/guide/spending/) changes status.
- **Getting around**: your default transit mode (Walk, Drive or Transit), and a distance under which Arrivo suggests walking.

Choose **Save** after changing preferences.

## Two-factor authentication

In **Security**, choose **Set up two-factor authentication** and scan the code with an authenticator app such as 1Password, Google Authenticator or Authy. After that, every sign-in asks for a code.

- Arrivo gives you **recovery codes**. Each one works once.
- On sign-in you can choose not to be asked again on that browser.
- You can turn two-factor off again from the same place.

**Your browsers** in Security lists the browsers you've signed in from. Remove any you don't recognize, which signs that browser out. **Sign out all devices** ends every session, including this one.

## Forgot your password?

Ask your Arrivo admin for a password reset link. Completing a reset removes your remembered browsers and keeps two-factor on. Self-hosters: see [Recovering an account](/arrivo/self-hosting/#recovering-an-account).
