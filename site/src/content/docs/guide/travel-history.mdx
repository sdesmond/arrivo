## Spending tab

Switch to the **Spending** tab for totals across your trips. Change the date range to recompute the headline figures, and use the charts to see spending by category and by destination, and how many trips finished under, on or over budget. Trips without a category or destination appear as **Unknown**. If you have no trips yet, the tab invites you to create one.
