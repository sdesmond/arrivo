## Budget tab

The **Budget** tab tracks one trip's costs. It shows what you've spent, your budget and what remains, plus a total for each category (Lodging, Flights, Food, Activity, Transport).

<Screenshot name="trip-budget" alt="The Budget tab with total spent, a warning badge, category totals and an expense list" />

- **Edit budget** sets the trip's budget.
- Add a cost to any event from the itinerary, or choose **Add Misc Expense** for something that isn't tied to an event.
- The status badge changes as you get close to the budget. The thresholds are yours to set in [Profile → Preferences](/arrivo/guide/account/#preferences) (**Warning %** and **Danger %**).

Viewers don't see the Budget tab. Editors and managers do.

## Spending across trips

**Travel history → Spending** summarizes spending across your trips, with a date filter, charts for categories and destinations, and how many trips came in under, on or over budget. See [Travel history](/arrivo/guide/travel-history/).
