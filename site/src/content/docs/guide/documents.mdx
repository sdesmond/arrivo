The **Documents** tab keeps a trip's files in one place. Choose **Add** to upload. Arrivo accepts PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC files up to 25 MB; other file types are rejected.

<Screenshot name="trip-documents" alt="The Documents tab with a PDF listed and view, share and delete buttons" />

For each document you can:

- **View** it in the in-app viewer. Press Escape to close it.
- **Rename** or **delete** it.
- **Search** the list by name.

You can also attach a document to a specific event or reservation, either by uploading it there or by picking one that is already in the trip's documents.

Documents are private to the trip: only people with access to the trip can open them.
