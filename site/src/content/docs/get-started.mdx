## 1. Sign in

Open the address of your Arrivo server. On a brand-new install, the first person to visit creates the admin account. Everyone else gets a setup link from an admin. Setting up a server? See [Self-hosting](/arrivo/self-hosting/).

<Screenshot name="sign-in" alt="The Arrivo sign-in page" />

## 2. Create a trip

On **Trips**, choose **New trip**. Only a title is required. A destination, a date range and a budget are optional, and you can add them later.

<Screenshot name="new-trip" alt="The New Trip dialog with title, destination, dates and budget fields" />

Arrivo opens the new trip with **Day 1** expanded.

## 3. Plan your days

Each day is a card. Open one to add events, places, notes and costs, and attach documents. Flights and hotels you enter under **Reservations** appear in the itinerary on their own. See [Day planning](/arrivo/guide/day-planning/).

<Screenshot
  name="trip-day-expanded"
  alt="An expanded itinerary day showing an event with time, place, cost and attachment options"
/>

## 4. Invite people

Choose **Share** on the trip, enter a username, pick a role, and invite. See [Sharing and roles](/arrivo/guide/sharing/).

<Screenshot name="trip-share" alt="The Share trip dialog listing collaborators and pending invitations" />

## What next

- Track what you spend in the [Budget tab](/arrivo/guide/spending/).
- Keep tickets and confirmations with the trip in [Documents](/arrivo/guide/documents/).
- Build a [packing list](/arrivo/guide/packing/) and reuse it as a [template](/arrivo/guide/templates/).
