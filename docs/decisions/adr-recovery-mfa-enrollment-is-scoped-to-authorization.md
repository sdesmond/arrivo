# Recovery MFA enrollment is scoped to its authorization

Opening or abandoning admin recovery must leave active MFA unchanged, while reloading a valid recovery context must preserve its replacement authenticator key. Store the encrypted pending MFA secret on the recovery authorization, creating it only when absent; verified completion atomically installs it on the account, clears the authorization copy, replaces password and recovery codes, and revokes prior authentication. This keeps enrollment separate from active and profile-pending credentials at the cost of authorization-schema state and strict completion cleanup; newly generated recovery codes are returned once and cannot be redisplayed after leaving the result.
